## FULL 28-SHOT FLOW PROMPT BLOCK

```
STYLE BIBLE: 3D CGI animated style, DreamWorks and Pixar theatrical animated
film quality. Characters have physical weight, real fur texture, personality-driven
expressions and proportions. NOT photorealistic. NOT flat 2D illustration.
Cinematic depth of field in wide shots. Volumetric lighting throughout. Premium
quality every shot.

CHARACTER REMINDER — SCOUT: blue hedgehog, braided dreadlocks with teal-blue and
burnt-orange beads, orange-brown leather hiking boots, bare blue paws, cream chest
patch, brown expressive eyes. Athletic and confident.

CHARACTER REMINDER — GRIMBLE: large round dark-purple storm cloud creature, grumpy
downturned mouth, narrow glowing white eyes, wiggly dark tentacle arms, purple
electricity at edges, small personal rain cloud above him raining only on him.

SHOT 1 — ESTABLISHING: SCOUT'S WORLD
Wide aerial establishing shot. Bright blue sky, fluffy white clouds, golden morning
light. Lush green rolling hills, winding river visible in the distance, an ancient
stone archway draped with ivy in the midground. Scout stands at the top of a grassy
hill facing the horizon — confident explorer posture. He holds a large glowing
GOLDEN EXPLORER MAP, warm amber-gold light emanating from its edges. His braided
dreadlocks sway gently in a morning breeze. Cinematic depth of field.

SHOT 2 — THE EXPLORER MAP (CLOSE-UP)
Close-up on Scout's bare blue paws holding the Golden Explorer Map open. The map
glows with warm amber-gold light. Intricate illustrated lines show rivers, mountains,
and forests — with a blank space at the center marked by a small question mark.
Scout's brown eyes reflect the map's golden glow. He traces a route with one finger,
expression filled with curiosity and excitement. Shallow depth of field on the map.

SHOT 3 — SCOUT SETS OFF
Scout steps forward confidently down the hill, braided locs bouncing. Bright blue
sky above. THE BOOT PRINT TRAIL activates: glowing golden boot-sole impressions
appear in the grass behind him as he walks — exact shape of his hiking boot soles —
each impression glows warmly for 2 seconds then fades. 3–4 prints visible at once,
trailing behind him. Wide shot showing the golden trail stretching back up the hill.

SHOT 4 — GRIMBLE APPEARS
The sky at the horizon darkens abruptly to deep purple. Grimble bursts from a
storm cloud on the right side of frame — large round dark-purple creature, grumpy
downturned mouth, narrow glowing white eyes, wiggly dark tentacle arms crackling
with purple electricity. His small personal rain cloud rains only on him in a tiny
vertical column. He looms toward Scout from the right. Deep purple storm atmosphere
rolls in behind him. Dramatic low-angle shot.

SHOT 5 — THE THEFT
Grimble's tentacle arm shoots out and snatches the Golden Explorer Map from Scout's
hands! The map's warm amber-gold glow flickers as it's grabbed. Scout reaches
forward in shock, brown eyes wide, braids flying outward. Grimble clutches the map
and rockets upward into the purple storm clouds above. The stolen map trails amber
light as it disappears into purple mist. Scout is left below, one arm still
outstretched. The sky around Scout dims.

SHOT 6 — DETERMINATION
Close-up on Scout's face. He watches the last of the amber glow disappear into the
purple clouds above. A beat of loss — then pure determination fills his brown eyes.
He squares his shoulders. THE EXPLORER PULSE fires: a warm golden compass-rose ring
expands outward from his chest — one clean pulse of amber-gold light, spreading to
arm's length then fading. One pulse only. His jaw is set. He is going after it.

SHOT 7 — THE CHASE BEGINS
Scout runs at full speed across an open landscape. Bright blue sky above, dark
purple storm clouds on the far horizon where Grimble fled. Dynamic low-angle shot:
Scout's hiking boots hit the ground in a powerful running stride. THE BOOT PRINT
TRAIL blazes brilliantly behind him — glowing golden boot-sole impressions appearing
with each footfall, 3–4 visible at once, fading in a glowing sequence. Braids
streaming back. Sense of urgent forward motion.

SHOT 8 — TRACKING
Scout reaches rocky terrain and slows to examine it. He crouches and studies the
ground — finds a faint purple lightning scorch mark burned into a stone, and a wisp
of purple storm cloud caught on a tree branch. He touches the lightning scar, eyes
narrowing with focus and intelligence. Then he looks up at the dark horizon,
nods once, and starts moving again. Midshot. Volumetric morning light.

SHOT 9 — CROSSING THE RIVER
A wide rushing river. Scout finds a line of stepping stones and doesn't hesitate —
he runs and leaps across them with athletic, confident grace. THE BOOT PRINT TRAIL
follows: golden boot-sole impressions appearing on each wet stone surface as he
lands, glowing then fading in sequence. Wide shot showing the river rushing below,
golden prints crossing the stones, dark storm clouds building on the far bank.

SHOT 10 — GRIMBLE ABOVE
Cut to Grimble, high above in his storm clouds. Deep purple atmosphere. He holds
the Golden Explorer Map with two tentacle arms and examines it with grumpy confusion
— holds it upside down, then sideways, tilting his round cloud body. His small
personal rain cloud drizzles steadily on him. He glances down and spots Scout still
chasing far below. His white eyes narrow with irritation. Purple electricity crackles
sharper at his edges.

SHOT 11 — PURPLE LIGHTNING STORM
Grimble conjures a purple lightning storm below to slow Scout down. Jagged purple
lightning strikes around Scout — not hitting him, but close. Scout runs through the
storm athletically, dodging, never stopping. His braids stream behind him. Close-up
on his boots: THE BOOT PRINT TRAIL glows brilliant warm gold against the dark
storm-lit ground — a beacon of warmth cutting through the purple storm. He does not
slow down.

SHOT 12 — THE CLIFF FACE
Scout reaches a steep dark stone cliff — slick with storm rain, Grimble's purple
storm clouds swirling above at its top. He examines it for handholds quickly, then
begins climbing without hesitation. Low-angle upshot: Scout climbing, boots finding
footholds in the stone, braided locs hanging away from his face as he looks up.
THE BOOT PRINT TRAIL appears even here — golden impressions pressed into the cliff
face where his boots grip. The cliff reaches up into purple mist above.

SHOT 13 — THE SUMMIT
Scout hauls himself up over the top of the cliff. He is now in Grimble's storm zone
— deep purple sky, heavy purple rain, purple lightning flashing at the edges. He
stands at the clifftop edge catching his breath, scanning the stormy landscape.
His teal-blue and burnt-orange beads are wet but still bright against the purple
atmosphere. He spots Grimble floating nearby, still puzzling over the stolen map.
Midshot, slight high-angle perspective.

SHOT 14 — APPROACH
Scout walks calmly and steadily toward Grimble through the rain. Grimble spots him
— white eyes going wide — and backs up slightly, clutching the map tighter with his
tentacle arms. Scout's posture is open and calm, not aggressive. He stops a few
feet from Grimble and holds up one bare blue paw — open, palm forward. Not a fist.
A gesture of peace. His brown eyes are warm. This is not a fight. Medium two-shot
framing both characters.

SHOT 15 — THE STANDOFF
Medium two-shot: Scout and Grimble facing each other in the purple storm rain. Scout
stands calm and still. Grimble is defensive — downturned mouth, tentacles pulled
close, white eyes uncertain. The rain from Grimble's personal cloud falls between
them. A long dramatic beat. Scout tilts his head gently — curious, patient, not
angry. Grimble blinks his white eyes slowly. The lightning around Grimble softens
very slightly. Something is shifting.

SHOT 16 — THE GOLDEN PENCIL
Scout slowly reaches into a pocket and produces a small golden pencil — warm and
bright against the purple storm atmosphere. He holds it up calmly so Grimble can
see. Not a weapon. A tool. Grimble's white eyes track the pencil cautiously. His
tentacles loosen their grip on the map. His white eyes have shifted from narrow and
suspicious to slightly wider — uncertain, but watching. Close-up insert of the
golden pencil glowing softly in Scout's blue paw.

SHOT 17 — DRAWING GRIMBLE
Scout takes the Golden Explorer Map gently from Grimble's loosened grip — Grimble
doesn't fight it — and opens it flat on a nearby boulder. He kneels and begins
drawing with the golden pencil. Close-up on the map: his hand moves with careful
precision. The camera slowly reveals what he's drawing — a large round cloud shape
with wiggly tentacle arms and two white circles for eyes. He is drawing Grimble.
He is adding Grimble to the map. Grimble watches from behind, white eyes wide.

SHOT 18 — THE MAP ACCEPTS GRIMBLE
Close-up on the map: Scout finishes the drawing of Grimble, and the illustration
glows with the same warm amber-gold light as the rest of the map — accepting it.
Beneath the drawing, Scout writes in careful letters: GRIMBLE'S STORM PEAK. The
entire map pulses warmly, brighter for a moment, as if agreeing. Scout sits back
and looks up at Grimble. The golden pencil rests in his paw.

SHOT 19 — GRIMBLE SEES HIMSELF
Scout holds the open map up toward Grimble. Grimble floats closer, white eyes
squinting at it — then going wide as he recognizes the drawing. It's him. His own
shape. On the map. Named. A place worth finding. His personal rain begins to
lighten — droplets becoming smaller and slower. His grumpy mouth softens — not
smiling yet, but no longer frowning. Close-up on Grimble's face: the white eyes
soft and still, reflecting the map's golden glow.

SHOT 20 — THE DISCOVERY GLOW
THE DISCOVERY GLOW activates on Scout: the teal-blue and burnt-orange beads in his
braided dreadlocks begin pulsing in sequence — teal glows, then orange, then teal,
then orange — a soft rhythmic pulse of warm light. 3–4 tiny golden sparkle dots
drift upward slowly from his braid tips like tiny rising stars. Scout smiles warmly
at Grimble. This is the breakthrough. Close-up on Scout's face and braids, golden
sparkles drifting up against the still-purple sky.

SHOT 21 — GRIMBLE BEGINS TO CHANGE
Wide shot: Grimble stares at his drawing on the map, then at Scout, then at the
map again. The heavy rain from his personal cloud lightens significantly — a
downpour becoming a drizzle. The jagged purple lightning at his edges softens to
smaller rounded sparks. His deep-purple color takes on a slightly warmer tone. The
sky at the horizon behind them begins shifting from deep purple toward a dusty pink.
The storm is not gone yet, but it is changing.

SHOT 22 — GRIMBLE TOUCHES THE MAP
Grimble extends one tentacle arm — slowly, gently — and touches the edge of the
Golden Explorer Map. Where his tentacle meets the map, a small warm golden glow
appears at the contact point. Grimble's white eyes are soft now, no longer narrow.
His tentacle traces the outline of his own drawing on the map. The rain has slowed
to almost nothing. The sky behind him has shifted to soft pink. Close-up on the
tentacle touching the glowing map.

SHOT 23 — THE RAIN CLOUD VANISHES
A magical moment: Grimble's small personal rain cloud above him slowly dissolves —
the grey mist fading away completely. Where it was, a single warm shaft of golden
light breaks through — a tiny personal sunbeam shining directly down on Grimble
alone. It illuminates him with warmth. His purple body takes on golden highlights.
His mouth curves upward — just slightly — the very beginning of a smile. The sky
behind him is fully soft pink now.

SHOT 24 — SIDE BY SIDE
Wide shot: Scout stands beside Grimble — not in front, not behind, perfectly side
by side — and both of them look out at the open horizon together. The sky ahead
is shifting from soft pink toward warm golden amber. Scout holds the folded golden
map carefully in both paws. Grimble's tiny personal sunbeam glows down on him
gently. They are equal in the frame. The moment breathes. Neither needs to speak.

SHOT 25 — THE FINAL EXPLORER PULSE
THE EXPLORER PULSE fires from Scout one last time — a warm golden compass-rose ring
expands slowly from his chest, one clean ring of amber-gold light, spreading outward
until it encompasses both Scout and Grimble before fading. A benediction. Grimble
watches the ring of golden light as it passes through him. He does not flinch —
he lets it happen. His white eyes are calm and soft. Midshot on both characters,
golden ring expanding between and beyond them.

SHOT 26 — WALKING HOME
Medium shot from behind: Scout and Grimble walk side by side across rolling hilltops
toward the bright horizon. Scout walks with his natural confident stride — THE BOOT
PRINT TRAIL glows golden behind him, warm prints appearing and fading in sequence.
Grimble floats at walking pace beside him, his tiny personal sunbeam moving with
him. The sky around them is bright blue — Scout's world. Safe. Warm. Wide open.

SHOT 27 — THE COMPLETED MAP
Close-up on the open Golden Explorer Map in Scout's hands as he walks. The camera
finds Grimble's Storm Peak on the map — the new drawing glowing with the same
amber-gold warmth as every other named place. The map is more complete now than
before Grimble took it. Every blank space is a little less blank. The map pulses
once with warm golden light.

SHOT 28 — THE WIDE WORLD
Epic wide aerial shot pulling slowly back. Scout and Grimble are two small figures
walking together along a ridge, the full landscape spread around them — the river,
the rocky terrain, the cliff face they crossed. Bright blue sky. Long golden
afternoon light. Grimble's tiny sunbeam glowing warm. THE BOOT PRINT TRAIL
glowing gold behind Scout. The horizon is wide and open — full of blank spaces
still to find. The golden map pulses one last time from Scout's hands.
Slow fade to warm golden light.
```
