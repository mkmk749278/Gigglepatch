# Episode 2 — "The Kite on the Wire"
### GigglePatch · Tara and the Star Lantern · Full 32-Shot Flow Block

**Runtime:** ~5:00 across 32 shots (8–10 sec each)
**Made for Kids:** YES

**Story:** Tara's kite catches on a wire high between two rooftops. Three things
will get it down — a long stick, a ball of string, a bent-wire hook. Rain starts
halfway and they have to *wait* rather than rush. Kaaki takes the string, and
three claps get it back. Then, at the end, Kaaki is the only one who can actually
reach the wire — and he brings the kite down himself.

**Why this episode second:** it repeats the format exactly so the rules stick,
and it turns the taker into the helper. Kaaki gives something back before anyone
asks him to.

SHOT 18 — WAITING OUT THE RAIN
Under the awning: Tara, Ravi and Chikoo sit in a dry patch with the long pole and

SHOT 19 — THE SECOND POINT LIGHTS

SHOT 20 — AFTER THE RAIN

SHOT 21 — STOP THREE: THE BANYAN COURT
Wide shot of the enormous banyan tree, aerial roots hanging down, the circular

SHOT 22 — THE HOOK

SHOT 23 — THE FEATHER TELL
Kaaki lands on a low banyan root just behind them, unnoticed. Plump comic body,

SHOT 24 — THE SNATCH
Kaaki hops down and grabs the ball of string, then flaps up to perch on a hanging

SHOT 27 — THE THIRD POINT LIGHTS

SHOT 29 — KAAKI UNDERSTANDS

SHOT 31 — THE GIFT
Sunset amber. Tara sets a small piece of something to eat down carefully on the

SHOT 32 — THE WIDE EVENING
