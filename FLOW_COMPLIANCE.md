## 2. ABSOLUTE PROHIBITIONS (NEVER DO)

These will get content removed and the account suspended.

### Characters & People
- ❌ Real people's likenesses — no celebrities, public figures, or private individuals depicted realistically
- ❌ Named fictional characters from other IP — no Sonic, no Disney characters, no Marvel, no Nintendo characters
- ❌ Copyrighted logos, brands, or trademarked symbols in frame
- ❌ Deepfakes or realistic impersonation of any real person

### Content Categories
- ❌ Violence that goes beyond cartoon/slapstick — no blood, no injury detail, no weapons used against characters
- ❌ Sexual or suggestive content of any kind
- ❌ Hate speech, harassment, or content targeting a group
- ❌ Self-harm depictions
- ❌ Terrorism or extremist content
- ❌ Child exploitation content (absolute zero tolerance)

### Music Prompts
- ❌ Reference to specific copyrighted songs by title ("make it sound like X song")
- ❌ Reference to specific artists by name ("in the style of [artist]") — describe mood and instruments instead
- ❌ Lyrics or vocal content that could resemble a copyrighted song

### Misrepresentation
- ❌ Claiming AI-generated content was created solely by a human
- ❌ Generating content designed to deceive viewers about its origin
- ❌ Using outputs to train other AI/ML models

## 3. PROMPT RULES — VIDEO (Google Flow)

### Always DO
- ✅ Describe Scout and Grimble by their visual attributes only — never reference Sonic or any existing IP by name
- ✅ Keep all action within cartoon/animated action-adventure norms — exaggerated but not graphic
- ✅ Use original color language (golden boot prints, amber-gold ring, teal-orange beads) — these are GigglePatch-original
- ✅ Specify "3D CGI animated style, DreamWorks/Pixar theatrical quality" every time — keeps it clearly animated, not realistic
- ✅ Set scene environments as original (Whispering Cliff, Echo Canyon, etc.) — never name real-world locations for dramatic scenarios

### Style Bible (required at top of every block)
```
STYLE BIBLE: 3D CGI animated style, DreamWorks and Pixar theatrical animated
film quality. Characters have physical weight, real fur texture,
personality-driven expressions and proportions. NOT photorealistic.
NOT flat 2D illustration. Cinematic depth of field in wide shots.
Volumetric lighting throughout. Premium quality every shot.
```

### Shot Prompt Safe Language
| Describe as | NOT as |
|-------------|--------|
| "blue hedgehog with braided dreadlocks and hiking boots" | "Sonic" or any named character |
| "large purple storm cloud creature with tentacle arms" | Any named IP villain |
| "cartoonish stumble" / "exaggerated tumble" | Injury, pain, damage |
| "bright golden glow" | Fire, explosions, weapons |
| "animated adventure forest" | Real-world place for a conflict scene |

## 4. PROMPT RULES — MUSIC (Flow Music / Lyria)

### Always DO
- ✅ Describe mood, tempo (BPM), and instrumentation — never name specific songs or artists
- ✅ Specify "No voiceover. No lyrics. Purely instrumental." in every prompt
- ✅ Specify "General audience — not a baby show" to get the right tone
- ✅ Give a time-coded emotional arc matched to the story beats
- ✅ Describe energy shifts (e.g., "builds at 1:20, peaks at 1:50, resolves at 2:30")

### Music Prompt Template
```
Purely instrumental. No lyrics. No voiceover. No vocals of any kind.
General audience — not a children's nursery rhyme show.

Genre: [cinematic adventure electronic / upbeat orchestral pop / etc.]
BPM: [target BPM]
Mood arc:
  0:00–0:30 — [opening mood]
  0:30–1:00 — [development]
  1:00–1:30 — [rising tension or energy]
  1:30–2:00 — [peak / climax]
  2:00–2:30 — [resolution / landing]

Instruments: [list specific instruments]
Avoid: [anything to exclude]
```

### Safe Language for Music Prompts
| Describe as | NOT as |
|-------------|--------|
| "fast electronic percussion with brass stabs" | "Like [song title]" |
| "heroic adventure orchestral swell" | "In the style of [composer/artist]" |
| "tension builds with rising strings and synth" | "Sounds like [movie soundtrack]" |
| "warm resolving chord progression in D major" | "Same vibe as [band]" |

## 5. YOUTUBE DISCLOSURE — REQUIRED

YouTube requires AI content labelling. Failure = demonetization or removal.

**On every GigglePatch video upload:**

In the YouTube Studio "Altered or synthetic content" toggle — **always select Yes** and check:
> "The video contains realistic altered or synthetic content — like AI-generated video, voices, or images — that could be mistaken for real people, places, events, or things."

**In the video description** — include a disclosure line:
```
✨ This video was created using AI video generation tools (Google Flow) and AI music (Flow Music/Lyria). All characters are original and fictional.
```

## 7. WHAT GIGGLEPATCH CANNOT COPYRIGHT

Under current US Copyright Office guidance (USCO):

- **Prompt-only AI outputs** — not copyrightable on their own
- **Raw Flow video clips** — not copyrightable as standalone clips
- **Raw Lyria music tracks** — not copyrightable as standalone tracks

**What IS protectable:**
- The final assembled and edited video (human creative direction, sequencing, timing decisions)
- The story, script, and shot-by-shot direction (the prompt blocks themselves are human-authored)
- The character designs as documented in CLAUDE.md (original creative work)
- The channel brand and visual identity
