## Story Summary — Episode 1

Kiran, 10-year-old fox explorer, can't sleep. Wanders the city at midnight. Discovers a hidden door glowing teal on an old wall — it only appears at midnight. A tiny translucent glowing spirit named Chimki floats out and invites Kiran inside. They enter the Midnight Market: a vast secret bazaar under a violet starlit sky where folklore creatures come to trade impossible things. Kiran sketches frantically. Then sees a lone glass jar on the cobblestones — something small and alive trapped inside. In the far distance: a tall thin figure in a coat of glowing jars moving through the crowd. Chimki pulls Kiran away. "Not yet." The door slams shut. Kiran is back in the normal world. Dawn light. Kiran stares at their fingertips — they still faintly glow teal.

**SHOT 01**
Wide establishing shot. Nighttime city street, old-world architecture, cobblestones, warm amber gas lamp light pooling on the ground. The street is empty and quiet. In the middle distance, a small figure: Kiran walking alone, reddish-orange fox tail visible, dark navy multi-pocket jacket, boots on cobblestones. The city feels sleeping, safe, ordinary. Camera is high and wide — Kiran is tiny in the frame. Deep indigo-blue night sky. No dialogue.

**SHOT 02**
Medium shot. Kiran pauses at a corner, looking up at a tall old stone wall ahead — layers of peeling paint, old posters, texture of many years. Kiran pulls the small sketch notebook from a lower jacket pocket and begins writing or sketching without looking down, eyes still fixed on the wall. Curious, alert expression. Ambient street lamp light from the left. Fluffy fox tail relaxed behind them.

**SHOT 03**
Close-up on Kiran's face. Amber eyes catching the lamp light — sharp, alert, the expression of someone who notices things other people miss. Something shifts in those eyes: from routine observation to alert curiosity. A faint teal tint enters the edge of the frame from off-screen. Kiran's ears rotate forward slightly.

**SHOT 04**
POV shot from Kiran's perspective. Looking across the alley at the old stone wall. Nothing unusual — just old stone, a horizontal crack, faded brickwork. But at the very edge of the frame, barely visible, the finest hair-thin line of teal light is starting to trace along a vertical edge of the wall. Easy to miss. The ordinary world on the verge of something.

**SHOT 05**
Wide overhead bird's-eye. The alley below — cobblestones, Kiran small in the frame near the wall. In the upper background of the frame, a stone clock tower is visible above rooftops. The clock face is lit warmly: the hands read 11:57. The city is still and silent. Only Kiran is awake.

**SHOT 06**
Close-up on the clock tower face. Warm amber lamp light illuminating the clock from below. The clock hands are at 11:58. The second hand moves. The number XII at the top. The sense of time running toward something. Quiet, patient.

**SHOT 07**
The old stone wall again, now slightly closer. A teal hairline line of light is tracing along the stone — slowly, deliberately — following the outline of what appears to be a doorway shape. The line is thin and faint but unmistakably there. The surrounding stone is untouched. The line moves like it is being drawn by an invisible hand.

**SHOT 08**
Medium shot. Kiran has stopped walking entirely. Head tilted, eyes narrowed. One boot forward, the other back — caught mid-step, frozen in recognition. The notebook is open, pen paused. Kiran is staring at the wall. Expression: I am seeing something real. Three steps closer.

**SHOT 09**
The wall — the teal outline is now nearly complete. An entire door shape: tall arched top, two side pillars, a lower threshold. The teal lines are carved-looking, ancient, with spiral and handprint etchings glowing faintly within the light. The glow is growing stronger. The ordinary stone wall around the door looks unchanged. Just the door. Just the teal.

**SHOT 10**
Extreme close-up on the clock tower. The hands: 11:59 and fifty seconds. The second hand sweeping the final arc. The XII glows warm. Time almost there.

**SHOT 11**
IMPACT SHOT. Midnight. The door on the wall ERUPTS into full existence — a brilliant teal radiance blazing outward along every carved line. The entire alley is flooded in teal-blue light. The door frame is ornate: spirals, moon phases, tiny handprints pressed into the surface, all glowing. The light is intense, beautiful, electric. Kiran is visible in the foreground — a small silhouette against the blazing door. Wide shot.

**SHOT 12**
Medium shot. Kiran stumbles back one step, free hand raised against the light. The notebook falls to the cobblestones — Kiran does not notice. Amber eyes wide open, catching the teal light and glowing with it. The expression is not fear. It is pure wonder. Fox ears pinned back then slowly rising. The most important thing Kiran has ever seen.

**SHOT 13**
Full-frame shot of the door magnificent. The teal door fills the centre of the frame, the carved patterns and spiral etchings brilliant and alive. Kiran stands small in the lower foreground, silhouetted. The light spills left and right across the cobblestones. The door is real and enormous and impossible. It has always been here, waiting.

**SHOT 14**
Extreme close-up on the door handle — a crescent moon carved in dark metal, warm teal light tracing its outline. The handle begins to turn by itself. Slowly, deliberately. The door is opening from the other side.

**SHOT 15**
The teal door swings open away from us. Beyond it: a wash of warm amber-gold light, the blur of impossible movement and colour — the first impression of the Midnight Market. Paper lanterns visible. The silhouettes of many creatures moving. The Market is enormous beyond what the door's size suggests. The door opens wider. The normal dark alley behind us; the extraordinary golden world ahead.

**SHOT 16**
Through the open doorway, a tiny glowing form drifts forward: Chimki. Palm-sized, translucent body with warm golden-white inner glow, floating in midair. Chimki moves from the bright Market side through the doorway and stops, hovering at face height. Large round eyes blinking at Kiran. The paper vest with tiny handwriting visible. The first creature from the other world. Expression: warm, delighted.

**SHOT 17**
Two-shot. Kiran and Chimki face to face for the first time. Chimki floating at Kiran's eye level. Kiran is completely still — notebook forgotten on the ground. Amber eyes wide. Chimki radiates warm golden light that illuminates Kiran's reddish-orange fur and the front of the navy jacket. The two look at each other. A meeting that was always going to happen.

**SHOT 18**
Medium shot from behind Kiran, looking toward the door and Chimki. Chimki moves backward slightly, floating, and gestures back through the open doorway toward the Market with both tiny arms — a clear invitation. Then Chimki taps a tiny wrist — a "hurry, not much time" gesture. The warm Market light beckons through the door.

**SHOT 19**
Cinematic low angle at cobblestone level. Kiran's worn brown boots step forward. One boot crosses the threshold of the glowing teal door frame, then the other. Teal light surrounds the frame like an energy field as Kiran passes through. The alley behind recedes. The Market ahead.

**SHOT 20**
WIDE REVEAL SHOT. The Midnight Market in full. Kiran and Chimki emerge into a vast open bazaar that stretches impossibly far in every direction. A deep violet sky overhead, blazing with stars and two moons. Hundreds of paper lanterns in amber and warm gold hang overhead in strands and clusters. Stalls and tents spread in every direction. Creatures of every kind — some small and fluffy, some tall and translucent, some made of smoke or crystal — move through the aisles, trading, talking, examining goods. Everything is alive, warm, extraordinary. Kiran stands at the edge, small in the frame, overwhelmed with wonder.

**SHOT 21**
Street-level medium shot. Kiran walks slowly into the Market, turning left and right, taking it all in. The fox tail swings behind in amazement. Nearby: a six-armed creature arranging scrolls on a wooden shelf; another creature holding up a small jar containing what appears to be a bottled sound — visible soundwaves inside. Chimki floats beside Kiran's right shoulder. Amber-gold lantern light warm on both of them.

**SHOT 22**
Close shot on a specific Market stall. Jars and containers arranged neatly: a jar containing a tiny thunderstorm (miniature lightning visible inside); a jar holding a single frozen sunset (orange light suspended); a small container of sleeping music (tiny musical notes drifting slowly inside). The stall vendor — a friendly round creature with many eyes — notices the viewer and gives a cheerful wave. Warm amber-gold lighting. Everything extraordinary but trading as naturally as vegetables at a market.

**SHOT 23**
Medium shot. Kiran stands still in the Market aisle, notebook open and pressed against the navy jacket front, pen moving fast across the page. Eyes darting from notebook to Market to notebook. The expression of someone who is recording as fast as their hands will allow. Chimki is perched on Kiran's left shoulder, peering at the notebook with interest.

**SHOT 24**
Close-up two-shot. The open notebook page — quick, energetic sketch lines of Market stalls taking shape, creatures being drawn mid-movement, jars being noted. Chimki's tiny translucent paw is visible at the top of the frame, pointing off to the right toward something specific we cannot yet see. The glow from Chimki's body illuminates the notebook page with warm gold light.

**SHOT 25**
Low angle ground-level shot. The cobblestones of the Market floor, in a quieter corner away from the main stalls. A single glass jar sits on the cobblestones, separated from any stall, as if dropped or left. It is small — about the size of a fist. Inside, something very small and alive pulses — a tiny light, organic, with a heartbeat rhythm. Trapped. The warm Market light around it makes it more isolated, not less. Kiran's boots are visible at the edge of the frame, stopped.

**SHOT 26**
Close-up on Kiran's face. Looking down at the jar. The amber eyes are softer now — the wonder shifting toward something else. Recognition that something is wrong. One hand begins to reach down, out of frame, toward the jar.

**SHOT 27**
Wide Market shot pulling slowly backward. Kiran crouching near the jar in the mid-ground. And in the far background, visible through the crowd of moving creatures, barely there — a tall thin figure in a long dark coat. The coat glitters and seems to chime faintly as the figure moves. The coat is covered, from collar to hem, with dozens of small glowing glass jars. Different lights. Different colours. The Collector moves slowly and deliberately through the Market crowd, far away. Not looking this way. Not yet.

**SHOT 28**
Final sequence. Chimki is at Kiran's ear — expression changed entirely, urgent, not cheerful. Chimki tugs at the collar of Kiran's navy jacket. Kiran turns away from the jar. Together they run through the Market crowd back toward the exit door — boots pounding cobblestones, Chimki's golden glow trailing behind them as they move. The door is still there, still teal. They reach it, dive through. CUT TO: The alley. Dark. The door slams shut behind them and vanishes — just an old stone wall again. Dawn light beginning, pale grey-blue at the edges of the sky above the rooftops. Kiran stands alone, breathing hard, looking at their own hands. On the tips of Kiran's fingers: the faintest trace of teal glow, slowly fading.
