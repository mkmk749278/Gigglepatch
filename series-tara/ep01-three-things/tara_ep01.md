# Episode 1 — "Three Things for Ammamma"
### GigglePatch · Tara and the Star Lantern · Full 32-Shot Flow Block

**Runtime:** ~5:00 across 32 shots (8–10 sec each)
**Made for Kids:** YES

**Story:** Ammamma is making sweets and needs three things. Tara, Ravi and Chikoo
go to fetch them — mangoes from the grove, water from the stepwell, jaggery from
the market. Nandu the buffalo blocks the lane and has to be *invited* past, not
pushed. Kaaki the crow takes the jaggery and is stopped by three claps. The
sweets get made. Then Tara leaves one on the wall for Kaaki.

**Why this episode first:** it teaches all three interactive rules in order —
count, plan, clap — and ends by giving something to the character who took.
